## Terrain Breakdown

Unlike rocky Himalayan trails, Kudremukh is all about rhythm over rolling laterite clay and grass. The opening three kilometers undulate across four clear mountain streams—your feet will get wet early, so breathable mesh uppers beat waterproof membranes here.

Once you clear the Ontimara (Lone Tree) landmark, the GPX trace tilts upward onto the main spine. The gradient rarely drops below 11% for the final two kilometers, cutting across wind-scoured grassland slopes with unobstructed views down to the Arabian Sea horizon on clear mornings.

## Why This GPX Track Stands Out

- **Zero flat recovery on the upper ridge**: Once you pass `1,450 m`, every hundred meters climbs steadily until the summit trig pillar.
- **Fast, technical descent**: The clay ruts require quick foot placement on the return leg, especially if morning mist dampens the grass.
