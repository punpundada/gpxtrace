## Pacing the Two Halves

Every cyclist and uphill runner around Bengaluru eventually measures their fitness against the Nandi monolith. Looking at the GPX elevation profile reveals why pacing goes wrong for first-timers: the opening 2.2 kilometers from the arch gate average a forgiving 5.5–6.5%, tempting you to burn matches early.

As soon as you pass the mid-mountain viewpoint, the road folds back on itself in a dense staircase of numbered hairpins. The inside line of each switchback spikes past 14%, so swing wide on the entries to keep your momentum and cadence smooth.

## Surface & Traffic Notes

- **Apex grip**: Early morning dew under the fig trees can leave the painted centerline slick—stay on clean asphalt when climbing out of the saddle.
- **Descent line**: Sightlines are tight between corners 28 and 16. Keep your fingers covering the brakes and watch for monkeys sunning on the warm stone parapet walls.
